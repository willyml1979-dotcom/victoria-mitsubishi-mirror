Premium comfort meets adventurous capability in our One Owner and Accident-Free 2025 Mitsubishi Outlander SE S-AWC in White Diamond! Powered by a 2.5L 4-cylinder engine delivering 181 horsepower and paired with a CVT featuring paddle shifters and multiple drive modes, this versatile SUV offers smooth performance and confident handling. Mitsubishi's Super All-Wheel Control (S-AWC) system helps provide added traction in changing road conditions, while highway fuel consumption is rated at approximately 7.9 L/100 km. Expressive and ready for adventure, our Outlander features full LED lighting, LED fog lamps, stylish 18-inch alloy wheels, a panoramic sunroof, heated power-folding mirrors, and a convenient hands-free power liftgate. Inside, the comfortable SE cabin offers heated front seats, versatile second- and third-row seating for up to seven passengers, a heated leather-wrapped steering wheel, three-zone automatic climate control, and FAST-Key keyless entry with push-button start. Stay connected with a 12.3-inch touchscreen, a 7-inch colour driver information display, wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, Bluetooth, voice recognition, and a Dynamic Sound Yamaha Premium audio system with eight speakers. Enjoy added peace of mind with advanced safety features, including Forward Collision Mitigation with Automatic Emergency Braking, Rear Automatic Emergency Braking, Blind Spot Warning, Lane Departure Warning, Rear Cross Traffic Alert, and more. With its modern technology, family-friendly versatility, and confident S-AWC capability, this Outlander SE is ready for your next adventure! Save this page and call for availability.
