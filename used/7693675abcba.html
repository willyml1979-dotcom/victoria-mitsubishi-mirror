Our One Owner and Accident Free 2024 Mitsubishi Outlander PHEV GT S-AWC explores with an athletic spirit and advanced plug-in hybrid technology in Cosmic Blue! Motivated by a 2.4 Litre 4 Cylinder engine paired with two electric motors, generating a combined 248 horsepower and 332 lb-ft of torque, this advanced powertrain delivers smooth, responsive performance. Plug in for an all-electric driving range of approximately 61 kilometers, or enjoy the flexibility of hybrid driving for longer journeys. Mitsubishi's Super All-Wheel Control (S-AWC) provides confident traction and control in changing road conditions. A striking front grille sets the stage for bold design with LED headlights, LED fog lamps, a stunning panoramic sunroof, hands-free power liftgate, 20-inch alloy wheels, silver skid-plate accents, and a sporty rear spoiler. Inside, the premium seven-passenger GT cabin offers leather-appointed seating, power front seats with memory, heated front and second-row seats, a heated leather-wrapped steering wheel, and three-zone automatic climate control. A convenient folding third row adds family-friendly versatility. Stay connected with the 12.3-inch digital driver display, 10.8-inch Head-Up Display projecting important driving information onto the windshield, and 9-inch touchscreen with embedded navigation. Additional features include Apple CarPlay, Android Auto, Bluetooth, wireless smartphone charging, and a premium 9-speaker Bose sound system. Enjoy added confidence with advanced safety technologies, including MI-PILOT Assist, Adaptive Cruise Control with Stop & Go, Active Blind Spot Assist, Lane Keep Assist, Forward Collision Mitigation, Rear Cross Traffic Alert, and a Multi-View Camera System. Combining electrified performance, premium comfort, impressive versatility, and advanced technology, this 2024 Mitsubishi Outlander PHEV GT S-AWC is an exceptional SUV for everyday driving and family adventures!
